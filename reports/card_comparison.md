# Card comparison

Train: 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25. Test: 2025-26.
Each training card is cut after 20%, 40%, 60%, or 80% of its fixtures,
chosen at random, starting from gameweek 1. The test scores all four cuts.
Every club and every row is included.

The reader is squad vision with squad coupling on the square card.
The odds row is sixteen squares. The venue mark is one square. Minutes
are six squares of fifteen minutes, filled from the bottom. One goal or
assist is one square, and those rows are three squares tall. The position
color is one square. Concede, regulars, own history, and opponent history
are absent from the picture and from the match-up. Each future fixture is
given a number of bars equal to the sum of its bar probabilities. Those
bars go to the rows with minutes and the highest probabilities.

A bar matches on the same row only. Within one gameweek includes the same
appearance and either neighboring gameweek. The numbers are totals.
