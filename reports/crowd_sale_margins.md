# Sale margins on the leading climbs

Gemini locked the trace before the margins were read ([sale margins](bc-b57f0f87-87e5-5037-b65f-a697e55bf2a9)). The four climbs are the ones that led their season: the template in 2022/23, 2023/24, and 2024/25, and Next in 2025/26. A sale is a played move from a legal squad. The margin is the published transfer value minus the hold. A legal squad needs 1.25 to move. The value is the discounted three-week eleven, net of the hit and the switch penalty.

The bar is loose only if the 2022/23 template second half has a median under 2.5 and its share under 2.5 is at least 20 percentage points higher than the 2023/24 template second half. The score was sure only if that median is at least 5 and the share under 2.5 is not 20 points higher. A half with fewer than 8 sales cannot fire either call. Anything else is inconclusive. 2024/25 and 2025/26 are reported beside the call. The hold margin stays 1.25.

Gemini kept the diagnostics ([sale margins](bc-b57f0f87-87e5-5037-b65f-a697e55bf2a9)). The call stays inconclusive. The 2022/23 second-half median is 4.47, short of 5 by 0.53. A hold of 2.5 would have removed 1 of 16 sales in that half and 4 of 13 in the 2023/24 second half. The hold margin stays 1.25.

Call: **inconclusive**.

## Reading

A tighter hold would take out more of the paying half than of the losing one. Those 2022/23 sales were not a squeak over 1.25. The median was also short of the locked line for calling the score sure.
