# Early buy

From Gameweek 2, a player with one or two appearances this season and expected minutes of at least 45 may be bought. His score is the published xp capped at 6. Three or more appearances stay uncapped. No appearance stays unbuyable. Gameweek 1 does not transfer. There is no fixture-difficulty term, no previous season, and no ownership.

Each season starts from the crowd template. The chip map is empty. The published template climbs are 1735, 2105, 2086, and 1988. The pool must reach 7948. At least 3 seasons must match or beat their climb. None may finish more than 30 behind.
