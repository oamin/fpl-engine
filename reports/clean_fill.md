# Clean fill

The eleven is the fast eleven: no squad is carried, and the total is the eleven plus the captain. The clean arm fills a missing prior from the previous season only. A matched player keeps that season's shifted prior. A debutant takes the position mean of the matched players' first rows. The published arm is the within-season fill already used for the fast eleven, on Gameweeks 5–8 only.

A season is kept when Gameweeks 1–8 trail Gameweeks 9–38 by at least 2 points a week and Gameweeks 5–8 trail the published eleven by at least 1 point a week. The hypothesis is kept when at least two seasons clear both.
