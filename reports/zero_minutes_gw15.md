# Named elevens who played zero minutes, Gameweeks 1–5

A starter is one of the eleven named before the deadline. For the model that is the intended eleven, before automatic substitutes. For a manager it is the submitted eleven, slot 11 or earlier. Minutes are the gameweek total on the roster.

Across the 14 locked managers, 770 slots a side:

| | model named | human named |
|---|---:|---:|
| played 0 minutes | 23 | 0 |
| played under 90 and scored 2 or fewer | 83 | 58 |
| played 90 or more and scored 2 or fewer | 201 | 202 |

The full-match blanks match. The zero-minute row does not. None of those 23 stayed in the scored eleven. Each was replaced. The substitutes scored 97 points over the 22 manager-weeks that had one of these starts. Adam Whitting's Gameweek 5 had two.

The 23 are six players. They are not one fault.

## João Pedro, Gameweek 5: 14 of the 23

Every model squad named him. The carried score was 5.30. He had played 90 minutes in each of Gameweeks 1–4, for 11, 9, 1, and 12. Gameweek 5 was his first zero. A score built from earlier minutes still starts him.

Seven of the 14 still owned him that week: Will Morrison, Paul Mitchell, Jess Bernstein, Adam Whitting, Sion Jones, Viniii Denie, and Tom Anderson. All seven left him on the bench. The other seven did not own him. That is team news. The sheets have no status, chance of playing, or news taken before the deadline, and that gap is already parked.

## Rico Lewis, the repeat: 3 of the 23

He played 56 minutes in Gameweek 1, then 0 in Gameweeks 2–5. Adam Whitting's model named him in Gameweeks 2, 3, 4, and 5, still on the Gameweek 1 score of 3.99.

Gameweek 2 is the first zero. It is not knowable at that deadline. Gameweeks 3–5 are. A row with 0 minutes is dropped before the priors are built, so an owned player who is missing from that week's score keeps the last score he had. The zeros never enter expected minutes. None of the 14 owned him.

The other seventeen played. Enzo missed Gameweek 2 and then played 75, 81, and 90. Elvedi missed Gameweek 1 and then played every week. A rule that treats one missed week as the next week's minutes would have cut both.

## What this does not change

The scored eleven did not keep a zero. The cost is the player a manager named instead, which the lineup column already counts. Writing a missed week into the next minutes prior would move Lewis from Gameweek 3. It would not have benched João Pedro. It is not a change to `score_xp`, and it is not fit on these five weeks.
